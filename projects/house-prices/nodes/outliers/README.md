# What to do with listings far off the city's price per square metre?

A listing whose price per square metre is ten times the city's level has a typo in its price
or its area, and nothing about the market. What bears on the choice: whether a correction
(rescale by 10 when the factor is clear) is better than dropping such a listing, and whether
a neighbourhood with few listings can afford to lose any of them. Keeping them all is the
option to beat: the answer says what each of the others buys.
